Hạn định từ

Chọn đáp án — hiển thị kết quả & giải thích ngay

Hạn định từ (Determiners)

  • Từ chỉ định: this/that (số ít), these/those (số nhiều).
  • Some — câu khẳng định; any — phủ định & câu hỏi thông thường.
  • a few / many + đếm được; a little / much + không đếm được.
  • few / little (không «a») = ít, không đủ · a few / a little = một chút, đủ dùng.
Bảng some / any / few / little (xem nhanh)
Loại câuDùngVí dụ
Khẳng địnhsomeI have some books.
Phủ địnhanyI don't have any books.
Câu hỏianyDo you have any pencils?
Đếm đượca few / fewa few friends vs few friends
Không đếm đượca little / littlea little water vs little water

Exercise 8 — Sửa lỗi some / any

Câu có thể sai — chọn câu viết lại đúng (hoặc «correct» nếu đúng).

  1. I'd like any water, please.
    A. I'd like any water, please.B. I'd like some water, please.C. correctD. I'd like many water, please.
  2. There aren't some emails for you.
    A. There aren't any emails for you.B. There aren't some emails for you.C. correctD. There isn't some emails for you.
  3. We've got any nice apples today.
    A. We've got any nice apples today.B. We've got some nice apples today.C. correctD. We've got much nice apples today.
  4. There are always any horses in the field near our house.
    A. There are always any horses in the field.B. There are always some horses in the field.C. correctD. There are always many horse in the field.

Exercise 9 — Few / Little vs A few / A little

Đọc tình huống — chọn câu diễn đạt đúng nhất.

  1. Ví dụ: Rosie vẫn đói, muốn thêm cơm.
    A. Rosie wanted a little more rice.B. Rosie wanted little more rice.
  2. Tình huống (1): I don't really like much salt on my food.
    A. I add a little salt to my food.B. I add little salt to my food.
  3. Tình huống (2): I have a long working day right ahead of me.
    A. I packed a few sandwiches.B. I packed few sandwiches.
  4. Tình huống (3): Mr. Hong mới đến Canada, hầu như không học tiếng Anh — chỉ biết «hello».
    A. Mr. Hong knows a little English.B. Mr. Hong knows little English.
  5. Tình huống (4): Mr. James hay la mắng — tính xấu, ít ai muốn làm việc cho ông ấy.
    A. A few people like working for him.B. Few people like working for him.

Exercise 10 — Sửa lỗi trong đoạn văn (Annabel)

  1. So, we have a little party every Saturday.
    A. little → fewB. little → manyC. Saturday → SaturdaysD. x (không lỗi)
  2. You know, just a few friends at our house. … a little party games.
    A. few friends → little friendsB. few friends → much friendsC. a lot of → any ofD. little party games → few party games
  3. It's not enough time and there are too much things that we need to do.
    A. too much → too littleB. too much → too manyC. not enough time → few timeD. x
  4. Come visit us — we know we won't have much friends in the States.
    A. much → manyB. much → littleC. much → someD. x
