Exercise 1 — Tính từ hay trạng từ?

Chọn dạng đúng theo vị trí trong câu.

  1. The meeting was ___ productive.
    A. extremeB. extremelyC. extremityD. extremes
  2. She is a ___ employee.
    A. hardB. hardlyC. harderD. hardness
  3. He speaks English ___.
    A. fluentB. fluentlyC. fluencyD. fluentness
  4. The presentation was very ___.
    A. impressB. impressedC. impressiveD. impressively
  5. Our sales increased ___ this year.
    A. significantB. significantlyC. significanceD. signify
  6. The new policy is ___.
    A. effectB. effectiveC. effectivelyD. effectiveness
  7. He completed the task ___.
    A. quickB. quicklyC. quicknessD. quicker
  8. This is a ___ opportunity.
    A. valueB. valuedC. valuableD. valuably
  9. The CEO arrived ___ for the meeting.
    A. lateB. latelyC. laterD. latest
  10. Sales have grown ___ over the years.
    A. steadyB. steadilyC. steadinessD. steadier
