Exercise 5 — Chọn dạng đúng của động từ

Chọn dạng động từ đúng để điền vào chỗ trống. Lưu ý: sau did/didn't dùng V nguyên mẫu.

VD1: I broke (break / broke) a cup yesterday. | VD2: Ann did not play (play / played).
  1. Harry ___________ (work / worked) last Sunday.
    A. workB. worked
  2. I didn't ___________ (know / knew) where I was.
    A. knowB. knew
  3. I didn't ___________ (feel / felt) well last night.
    A. feelB. felt
  4. Alina ___________ (come / came) to see us 2 days ago.
    A. comeB. came
  5. I didn't ___________ (see / saw) Bill at the party.
    A. seeB. saw
  6. Did Theo ___________ (bring / brought) his wife with him?
    A. bringB. brought
  7. Rolf ___________ (learn / learnt) English when he was young.
    A. learnB. learnt
  8. When did Gemma ___________ (start / starts) school?
    A. startB. starts

Exercise 6 — Điền dạng đúng (hỗn hợp thì)

Chọn dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc để điền vào chỗ trống.

  1. a. I ___________ (see) Maria for the first time at Ray's birthday party.
    A. sawB. seeC. have seenD. sees
  2. a. We ___________ (become) best friends and we usually watch movies together every Sunday evening.
    A. becomeB. becameC. have becomeD. becomes
  3. b. ...but last Saturday he ___________ (eat) a bowl of noodles.
    A. eatsB. ateC. has eatenD. eat
  4. c. Last year, Denise ___________ (practice) this song every day.
    A. practisedB. practicesC. is practisingD. practice
  5. d. In the past, many people ___________ (not believe) that the Earth wasn't the center.
    A. don't believeB. did not believeC. haven't believedD. aren't believing
  6. e. When he ___________ (be) a child, he got up at 6 a.m every morning.
    A. wasB. wereC. isD. be

Exercise 7 — Sắp xếp từ thành câu hoàn chỉnh

Chọn câu được sắp xếp đúng từ các từ gợi ý.

Lưu ý: Chia đúng thì quá khứ đơn.
  1. What / you / do / yesterday?
    A. What you did yesterday?B. What did you do yesterday?C. What did you did yesterday?D. What you do yesterday?
  2. He / tell / his friends / the story of his dog / 2 days ago.
    A. He told his friends the story of his dog 2 days ago.B. He telled his friends the story of his dog 2 days ago.C. He tells his friends the story of his dog 2 days ago.D. He did tell his friends the story of his dog 2 days ago.
  3. Where / you / go / last summer holiday?
    A. Where you went last summer holiday?B. Where did you went last summer holiday?C. Where did you go last summer holiday?D. Where go you last summer holiday?
  4. He / not / make / the mistakes that night.
    A. He didn't make the mistakes that night.B. He didn't made the mistakes that night.C. He not made the mistakes that night.D. He don't make the mistakes that night.

Exercise 8 — Hoàn thành đoạn văn

Điền đúng thì quá khứ đơn của động từ để hoàn thành đoạn văn về kỳ nghỉ.

    A. wasB. wereC. isD. been
  2. ...because I ___________ (live) there when I was at university.
    A. liveB. livedC. was livingD. have lived
  3. I ___________ (want) to do all the same things I did during my university years.
    A. wantB. wantingC. wantedD. was wanted
  4. so I ___________ (visit) the university.
    A. visitB. visitedC. was visitingD. visits
  5. I also ___________ (go) to the supermarket near my old house.
    A. wentB. goC. goneD. goed
  6. I ___________ (take) the bus and ran across the historic Charles Bridge.
    A. takeB. takenC. tookD. taked
